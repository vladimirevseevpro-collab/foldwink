# Foldwink CQ6 German Replacement Review

**Scope:** independent German native-language and fairness review of the eight
draft boards in `puzzles/_drafts/cq6-de-replacements/` on 2026-07-23. The
active pool was not reviewed or changed.

**Approval rule:** a board is `keep` only when its groups are ordinary,
natural German categories, require no specialist knowledge, and leave no
equally defensible alternative group of four. `revise` means the board has a
specific editorial defect but can become publishable after a targeted rewrite.

**Mobile criterion:** all card values are within the project's 2--22 character
guideline. The current card component reduces type at 14 and 19 characters and
allows wrapping, so a two-line value is acceptable when it remains readily
readable. Labels are assessed separately as revealed result text.

| Draft | Verdict | Native-language and fairness review | Mobile review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `de-0308` -- *Garten im Sommer* | **keep** | All four categories are everyday and exclusive in play: tools, flowers, fruit, and familiar garden insects. `Kleine Gartenbesucher` is natural, welcoming German; the listed insects do not create an alternative four-card category with another group. | Longest card: `Schmetterling` (13). Clear at the standard small-card size. |
| `de-0311` -- *Frühstück daheim* | **keep** | `Frühstücksgetränke`, `Backwaren`, `Brotaufstriche`, and table settings are idiomatic, familiar categories. `Milch` is also a food in the broadest sense, but it does not create a competing four-card grouping; the intended drinks remain immediately legible. | Longest card: `Frischkäse` (10). No mobile concern. |
| `de-0313` -- *Kochen zu Hause* | **keep** | The intended partitions are clear to a general German audience. `Sieb` can broadly be called kitchen equipment and `Zimt` is often used for baking, but neither allows a second equally strong set of four; `Kochgeschirr`, utensils, spices, and baking ingredients remain canonical. | Longest card: `Pfannenwender` (13). No mobile concern. |
| `de-0317` -- *Kleidung für draußen* | **revise** | The group memberships are mostly clear, but `Beinkleider` is noticeably stiff and non-idiomatic for a contemporary everyday puzzle. More importantly, `Hausschuh` conflicts with the promise of clothing *für draußen*. Keep the partition only after using an idiomatic group label (for example `Unterteile`) and either retitling the board more broadly or replacing `Hausschuh` with outdoor footwear. | Longest card: `Stirnband` / `Hausschuh` (9). No sizing issue. |
| `de-0325` -- *Auf dem Wochenmarkt* | **keep** | Vegetables, fruit, culinary herbs, and flowers are ordinary market categories. `Kartoffel` is unambiguously a vegetable in everyday usage; no botanical knowledge is needed. The herbs do not offer a plausible competing group with the vegetables. | Longest card: `Schnittlauch` (12). No mobile concern. |
| `de-0331` -- *Sauber machen* | **keep** | The four groups form a defensible household partition: cleaning implements, cleaning agents, laundry items, and waste containers. `Waschmittel` is naturally both a laundry product and a cleaning agent, but this is a one-item broad-overlap, not an alternative four-card solution. `Glascontainer` is a valid waste container despite usually standing outdoors. | `Allzweckreiniger` (16) will wrap on narrow phones but remains under the UI's compact-text threshold and within the card height. Other values are shorter. |
| `de-0332` -- *Im Klassenzimmer* | **keep** | All categories use familiar school vocabulary. `Kreide` is naturally accepted as a writing implement in a classroom context, and `Garderobe` is a normal classroom fixture. There is no rival four-card classification of equal strength. | Longest card: `Wörterbuch` / `Mathematik` (10). No mobile concern. |
| `de-0339` -- *Packen für die Reise* | **revise** | `Für unterwegs` is a catch-all, not an exclusive category: travel documents, clothes, and hygiene items can all be taken unterwegs as well. This makes the fourth solution feel arbitrary even though its four listed objects are plausible travel items. Replace the whole group with a narrower, everyday category that does not overlap the other three (for example, a clearly bounded electronics/accessories set); do not merely rename it. | `Buchungsbestätigung` (19) triggers the smallest card type and will wrap to two lines. It is technically readable, but should be rechecked visually after the required group rewrite. |

## Decision

Approved for the next content-pipeline stage: `de-0308`, `de-0311`,
`de-0313`, `de-0325`, `de-0331`, and `de-0332`.

Held for editorial revision: `de-0317` and `de-0339`.

Rejected: none.
