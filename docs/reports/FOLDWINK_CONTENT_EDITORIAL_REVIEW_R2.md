# Foldwink Content Editorial Review R2

Date: 2026-07-23  
Scope: active EN, RU and DE pools only, after the R1 retirements  
Decision vocabulary: **KEEP** = safe at its current tier; **REVISE** = remove from rotation until the stated change is made and re-reviewed; **REJECT** = retire rather than patch one or two cards.

## Method and standard

This is the second deliberately narrow editorial pass. It selects the twelve highest-risk remaining records surfaced by the validator and by a human read of their card sets. The selection gives priority to:

1. easy puzzles that require specialist knowledge or contain long, mobile-hostile cards;
2. groups built on overlapping axes rather than four mutually exclusive, natural categories;
3. fact-sensitive medical, political, legal or historical taxonomies;
4. titles, labels or terms that are incomplete enough to create a second defensible answer.

The validator is a queue, not a verdict: an over-22-character item alone is not a failure. Here it matters when it occurs together with a fairness or language problem. R1 decisions and records already in retirement archives are excluded.

| EN `puzzle-0310` — *Bird Behaviour* | easy | long species name; classification overlap | **REJECT** | `Birds of Prey`, `Migratory Birds` and `Birds Known for Song` are real properties of the same birds, not exclusive classes. The player must also identify uncommon species such as `Bar-Tailed Godwit` and `Ruby-Throated Hummingbird`. That is specialist bird knowledge in an easy board. | Retire. Re-author with one exclusive axis and everyday names only; a future animal board must not sort the same animals by overlapping behaviours. |
| EN `puzzle-0314` — *Board Game Mechanics* | easy | niche taxonomy; long card | **REJECT** | The board assumes knowledge of sixteen hobby titles and of mechanics that games may share. A player cannot infer `Worker Placement` or `Area Control` from a title, and those mechanics are not mutually exclusive in the hobby. | Retire rather than promote. Re-entry would require a single, public-facing classification with recognisable evidence on every card, not a mechanic taxonomy. |
| EN `puzzle-0327` — *Transport Propulsion* | medium | technical edge case; false classification axis | **REJECT** | The editorial summary itself requires a technical explanation to separate `Diesel Truck` from `Diesel-Electric Locomotive`. The intended answer depends on an unstated choice of what counts as “primary” propulsion; the `Battery` hint is also misleading for a diesel-electric locomotive. | Retire. Do not repair by swapping only the locomotive: rebuild from visibly exclusive transport types, or omit propulsion as a Foldwink theme. |
| EN `puzzle-0368` — *Political Systems* | medium | overlapping political taxonomy | **REJECT** | The rows mix regime type, constitutional structure, participation method and a religious attribute. `Republic`, `Democracy`, `Federal State`, `Parliamentary` and `State Religion` can describe the same country at once; `Parliamentary` and `Ecclesiastical` are also incomplete noun phrases. | Retire. Any future civics board must use one explicit axis, fact-check each term, and avoid asking players to reduce real states to one label. |
| EN `puzzle-0389` — *Types of Election* | easy | specialist terms; non-parallel groups | **REJECT** | Electoral systems, counting methods, timing, vacancy procedures and referendums are different levels of description. `Single Transferable Vote`, `Ranked Choice` and `Runoff` are especially likely to be understood as competing versions of the same idea. The record’s own summary advertises a false trail despite the easy tier. | Retire. Re-author only with one electoral axis and a specialist review; it must not return as easy content. |
| RU `ru-0335` — *Морские течения* | easy | long cards; technical abbreviations; overlapping axes | **REJECT** | The board calls itself easy but contains `NADW`, an abbreviated `Термохалин. цирк.` and highly specific oceanographic names. “Тёплые”, “холодные”, “глубинные” and “муссонные” are properties, not peer exclusive classes. | Retire. A future ocean board should use one simple, non-overlapping everyday axis; no abbreviations, truncated labels or research terminology. |
| RU `ru-0416` — *Виды договоров* | medium | nested legal categories | **REJECT** | `Займ`, `Кредитный договор`, `Лизинг` and `Факторинг` can all be discussed under civil-law or financial lenses; `Срочный трудовой договор` is a subtype of `Трудовой договор`. `Контракт` is too generic to anchor a separate legal group. | Retire. Do not fix by renaming rows. A replacement needs a single jurisdiction and one non-overlapping legal classification, reviewed by a qualified legal editor. |
| RU `ru-0445` — *Психические расстройства* | medium | medical accuracy; long sensitive terms | **REJECT** | This turns clinical terminology into a casual matching game while mixing diagnoses, symptom-level shorthand and non-diagnostic words such as `Нарциссизм`. It places obsessive-compulsive disorder among anxiety disorders, although ICD-11 has a separate obsessive-compulsive and related-disorders grouping. The labels `Личность`, `Психозы`, `Паническое` and `Параноидное` are not parallel patient-facing categories. | Retire permanently from the game pool. Do not replace it with another diagnostic taxonomy without a medical editorial policy and clinical review. WHO’s ICD-11 material distinguishes obsessive-compulsive disorders from anxiety and fear-related disorders. [WHO ICD-11 clinical descriptions](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/375767/9789240077263-eng.pdf) |
| DE `de-0242` — *Deutsche Filmklassiker* | easy | inaccurate/incomplete titles; unstable period labels | **REJECT** | `Angst essen Seele` is an incomplete title: the official title is *Angst essen Seele auf*. The board also combines a broad period label, `Neue Welle Filme`, with the arbitrary moving target `Gegenwartsfilme`; the groups require substantial film-history knowledge despite the easy tier. | Retire. A replacement needs canonical full titles, one documented film-history period per row and a medium-or-higher difficulty review. Filmportal records the full title, and Deutsche Kinemathek uses the established term “New German Cinema”. [filmportal.de](https://www.filmportal.de/film/angst-essen-seele-auf_473c7a6f625e4f92a913f1106e1c8c70), [Deutsche Kinemathek](https://www.deutsche-kinemathek.de/en/visit/festivals-symposiums/germany-1966-redefining-cinema) |
| DE `de-0254` — *Museen Deutschland* | easy | long/incomplete institution names; overlap risk | **REJECT** | The cards are fragments of institution names (`Pinakothek`, `Senckenberg`, `Deutsches Historisches`, `Auto & Technik`) rather than stable common nouns. Real museums can span art, history, science and technology, so the four labels do not give a player one clean classification rule. | Retire. Rebuild as a geography or everyday-object board; do not classify individual institutions by a single collection area. |
| RU `ru-0255` — *Картины* | easy | long titles; ambiguous short titles; wrong tier | **REVISE** | The author and movement rows are conceptually clean, but the board asks the player to identify sixteen artworks. `Сон` and `Метаморфоза` are too short to identify one work without an author, while several card titles are too long for the mobile grid. This is not easy-level recognition. | Remove from easy rotation. Promote to **medium** only after replacing ambiguous titles with unique, canonical, mobile-length titles and adding four useful, non-giveaway Wink hints. Re-read every title at 390 px before restoring it. |
| DE `de-0092` — *Deutsche Dichter* | easy | long card; shortened/incorrect work names; wrong tier | **REVISE** | The board relies on literary-canon recall rather than ordinary language. Several titles are shortened (`Mutter Courage`, `Kreidekreis`, `Galilei`) and `Die Lore-Ley` should be checked against the canonical spelling *Die Loreley*. Shortened titles invite ambiguity across works and adaptations. | Remove from easy rotation. Promote to **medium** only after a German-language editorial pass supplies canonical, unambiguous titles that fit the card limit and short author-based Wink hints. |

## Result

- Reviewed: **12** active records, across all three language pools.
- **REJECT:** 10 records. Their problems are structural: a tier change or one word replacement would leave a second plausible grouping, a factual risk, or an inaccessible specialist test.
- **REVISE:** 2 records. Both can return only after the stated title-level repairs, a tier change to medium, and a mobile read-through.
- **KEEP:** none in this risk-selected sample. This is expected: R2 is a worst-first queue, not a representative quality sample.
