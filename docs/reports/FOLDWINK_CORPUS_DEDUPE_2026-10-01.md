# Foldwink Corpus Dedupe — 2026-10-01

## Sprint Summary

The three active language pools (EN `puzzles/pool`, RU `puzzles/ru/pool`,
DE `puzzles/de/pool`) each declared 1000 boards but held roughly 500 unique
ones. The remainder were exact recombinations or verbatim copies of groups
(for RU a single group appeared in 501 boards). The existing validator did
not detect this.

This wave adds a corpus-uniqueness gate, quarantines the duplicate boards,
rebuilds runtime content, and removes the hard-coded `1000` expectations so
tests and the runtime builder derive their numbers from the actual pool.

Duplicate definition (agreed contract): normalize a group to the frozenset of
its lowercased, trimmed item strings; process puzzles in ascending `id` order;
a puzzle is a **duplicate** when at least 3 of its 4 normalized groups already
occur in an **earlier** puzzle of the same language.

## Kept-because-referenced duplicates

**None.** No definition duplicate had an `id` referenced by
`puzzles/manifests/*.json` or by any file under `src/` or `tests/`, so no
duplicate had to be retained. (Referenced boards such as `puzzle-0001`,
`puzzle-0004`, `hard-024`, `de-0042`, `ru-0042`, `ru-0043` were all unique and
stayed in place.)

## Cap safety pass

`check-corpus-uniqueness.mjs` also fails when any single group appears in more
than 3 boards. After the definition-duplicate removal, two DE groups still sat
at 4 occurrences across distinct, non-duplicate boards. To satisfy the gate the
quarantine moves the latest non-referenced occurrence of each over-reused
group:

- `de-0494` — group `bluse / hemd / pullover / t-shirt`
- `de-0433` — group `mundspülung / zahnbürste / zahnpasta / zahnseide`

Both were non-referenced and are quarantined alongside the definition
duplicates. No EN or RU board needed the cap pass.

## Open risks

- The cleanliness of the retained pools is only as good as the agreed
  normalization (lowercase + trim, exact item set). Near-duplicates that differ
  in wording remain undetected by design.
- `check-corpus-uniqueness.mjs` reads the source pools only. Generated
  `public/runtime-content/v1` is validated separately by `content:check`.
- Quarantined boards are preserved under `puzzles/_retired/dup-quarantine/`
  rather than deleted, so the history is auditable and reversible.
